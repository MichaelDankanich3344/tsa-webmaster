Module 04: AI ethics, lesson 2 of 3

Using AI appropriately

Success criteria: Handle ethical dilemmas about when and how to use AI.

AI can be a helpful assistant for many different purposes, but it should be used as an assistant, not as a decision maker. Because it can be wrong or biased, it works best for things like double-checking your answers.

It's important to keep a person in charge of important decisions, such as hiring, people's health, and how a business is run. It's also important to protect your private information when talking to an AI.

What is the appropriate way to use AI?

AI should support people, not replace their judgment. Important decisions need a person in charge.
