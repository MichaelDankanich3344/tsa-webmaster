How do you classify AI?

Sucess Criteria: Learn how to properly assess functionality and capablilites of an AI to properly identify its correct labels.

When classifying AI, people use two different categories, functionality and capability. Functionality is how the AI operates, while capability is the scope and adaptability of its intelligence. When classifying based on capability you use Artificial Narrow Intelligence, Artificial General Intelligence, and Artificial Superintelligence. When you measure based on functionality, you sort based on if the AI is a reactive machine, limited memory, theory of mind, or self-aware AI. These aren't mutually exclusive types, as an AI can be classified as both Narrow AI with characteristics of limited memory AI.
