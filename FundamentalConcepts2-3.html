Computer vision

Success criteria: Understand what computer vision, image recognition and object detection are.

Computer vision lets computers look at photos and videos and understand them. Humans use their eyes and brains to spot objects; computer vision uses cameras, image data and AI models to do the same.

Image recognition

Image recognition (also called image classification) looks at a whole image and puts it into a category. Examples include:

  • Deciding if an image shows a cat or a dog
  • Recognizing kinds of plants
  • Reading handwritten numbers

Object detection

Object detection finds which objects are in an image and marks where they are. It usually draws boxes around them called bounding boxes, and each detected object gets its own label and box. Examples include:

  • Spotting cars, bicycles and people in traffic
  • Detecting products on a conveyor belt
  • Finding animals in wildlife footage

Check your understanding

An AI draws a box around every car and person in a traffic photo. What is it doing?

Object detection finds each object, labels it and marks where it is with a bounding box. Image classification gives just one label for the whole picture.
