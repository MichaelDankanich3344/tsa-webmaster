Bias in AI

Success criteria: Handle ethical dilemmas about bias.

AI is now part of daily life, from cars to phones. But it isn't perfect: because it learns from data made by people, it can pick up one-sided patterns in areas like hiring, loans or facial recognition.

These biases can be hard to notice, because AI usually answers in a confident, neutral tone. That can lead people to believe something false about a topic. Always double-check the sources and information an AI gives you.

Check your understanding

What is bias in AI?

Bias in AI is a systematic favoring of certain outcomes over others, often learned from one-sided training data.
