Generative AI

Success criteria: Understand generative AI and how it works.

Generative AI creates new, original content like text, images, audio or code from a prompt by using patterns it learned from data. It learns from existing datasets, then tries to produce output similar to what it learned, shaped by what the prompt describes.

Key technologies behind generative AI

Neural networks
Layers of connected nodes that process input data and learn patterns during training.
Transformers
Use a technique called self-attention to understand how the parts of a sequence relate to each other, so the output fits the context.
Generative adversarial networks (GANs)
Two neural networks working against each other: a generator creates fake data and a discriminator judges it. Through repeated rounds of feedback, the generator gets better.
Variational autoencoders (VAEs)
Create new data from old data. They compress it, rebuild it, then generate new variations on top of it.
Diffusion models
Use two processes. Forward diffusion slowly adds noise to training data until it is pure noise. Reverse diffusion starts from random noise and gradually removes it to create a new image or other output.
Large language models (LLMs)
Models that use neural networks trained on text to understand and generate human-like language. They can answer questions, write code and translate.

Check your understanding

Which technology uses two neural networks, a generator and a discriminator?

GANs pair a generator that makes data with a discriminator that judges it. The competition between them improves the generator.

Types of generative AI

Text generation
Generates text to answer questions, summarize articles, write documents and more.
Image creation
Generates images, such as logos, artwork and illustrations, from a prompt.
Video making
Generates videos from a prompt, or edits existing clips.
3D design
Creates 3D models or environments. A game developer could ask for a sword model, or a home seller could turn photos into a virtual walkthrough of the house.
Code writing
Writes code, debugs it, explains it and optimizes existing code.
Audio and music
Creates music, sound effects, speech and voices.
Customer service
Many websites add an AI assistant that answers questions about their business. Amazon, for example, has one that answers product questions and summarizes reviews.
