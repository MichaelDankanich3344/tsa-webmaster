AI automation

Success criteria: Understand AI automation and how it's used in the workplace today.

AI automation means using AI to complete tasks automatically. It can boost efficiency by handling repetitive work, or help with almost any task we want done. For instance, some businesses now use AI to interview job applicants instead of having a person do it.

Kinds of automation tools

Task automation
Handles routine work like sorting emails and scheduling.
Decision automation
Makes or recommends decisions based on data: detecting fraud, forecasting demand or setting prices that change with demand.
Cognitive automation
Interprets languages and documents, for summarizing and translating.
Autonomous systems
Do all of the above with little or no human involvement, as in self-driving cars, warehouse robots, drones and trading agents.

Where AI automation is used

AI is used in many other fields too. These are just a few examples.

Financial services
Detects fraud and monitors transactions to flag suspicious activity, improving security and reducing losses.
Healthcare
Helps with diagnosis and analyzes patient data to suggest better-informed treatment options.
Construction
Helps manage the resources a project needs and watches the site for hazards to workers.
Manufacturing
Analyzes data to recommend efficient orders and track inventory.
Human resources
Scans resumes to find people who might fit a job, and runs mock interviews where the interviewer is an AI.
Software development
Optimizes code, handles repetitive tasks and explains sections of code.
