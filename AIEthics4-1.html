Module 04: AI ethics, lesson 1 of 3

Who is responsible for AI decisions?

Success criteria: Handle ethical dilemmas about accountability.

If AI gets something wrong, it's not just one person's fault. The people who build it should try to make sure it works correctly, but the people using it have to be careful too.

AI makes mistakes every day and doesn't always give correct answers, so you can't just trust everything it says. Check things yourself before you use the information.

Who is held responsible for AI decisions?

Responsibility is shared: developers should build systems that work correctly, and users should check what AI tells them before acting on it.
